## Proxy threshold events and bootstrap

Training-derived proxy excursion labels are not component failures or certified thresholds. Seasonal naive has engine-temperature F1 0.372; seasonal naive and persistence have tyre-pressure F1 0.893 and 0.879. The tyre-temperature Bi-LSTM F1 is 0.802, but false-positive rate is 1.0 and specificity is 0.0, so it is not a useful discriminative detector.

For the predeclared lowest-MAE baseline versus Bi-LSTM, 2,000-replication non-circular moving-block bootstrap MAE(baseline) − MAE(Bi-LSTM) intervals are [−5.033, −3.955] °C (engine, RF), [−0.845, −0.356] PSI (pressure, persistence), and [−3.732, −3.392] °C (tyre temperature, RF). All exclude zero, but are dependence-aware intervals rather than i.i.d. tests or evidence of maintenance benefit.

## C-MAPSS forecasting and stage results

NASA C-MAPSS FD001 is a simulator-generated turbofan-degradation benchmark, not measured commercial-flight telemetry. This study forecasts observed sensors; it does not estimate RUL. C1 macro normalized MAE/RMSE/R² is 0.03406/0.04278/0.88907 for Random Forest, 0.03460/0.04341/0.88620 for LSTM, and 0.03483/0.04366/0.88536 for Bi-LSTM seed 42. These are fixed-configuration results, not test-driven tuning.

The accepted stage analysis stratifies held-out sensor-forecast error using fixed post-forecast early (<0.5), mid (0.5 to <0.8), and late (≥0.8) normalized-life bins. It reports 132 per-artifact metrics and does not create an RUL target. Its inherited forecast-environment deviation remains attached to provenance.
